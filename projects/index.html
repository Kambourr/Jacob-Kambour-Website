Etch Portfolio Spend Forecast↗

Financial modeling / Excel

Etch Portfolio Spend Forecast

An Excel model and a web app that forecast a fiscal year of Opex and Capex for Etch R&D programs. Enter programs, drivers, an AOP and actuals, and it blends a rolling forecast, bridges it to the budget, tests a funding cut and a schedule slip, follows Capex into depreciation, runs 11 checks, and exports a live-formula Excel workbook. Illustrative numbers.
